## Context

What is true today that makes this decision necessary. Constraints that cannot be
changed (stack, deployment model, team size, platform limits). Cite real files,
config fields, or measured observations — not intentions.

## Problem

One question, phrased so it can be answered. If you cannot state it in a sentence, the
decision is not ready.

## Considered alternatives

At least three, including:

1. **Do nothing** — consequences of leaving the system as it is.
2. **The obvious option** — what a newcomer would reach for first.
3. **The chosen option** — with the reasoning that beat the others.

For each: one paragraph, plus effort and reversibility.
